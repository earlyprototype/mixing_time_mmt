## Cross-track Criterion III transform

OLS fit ab = a * pra + b across rooms 1 to 8: a = 1.581, b = 1717.6, R2 = 0.825, Spearman rho = 0.929 (p = 0.0009).

## Observations

The early textures differ mainly in sparsity, not bandwidth: every ab RIR keeps 129 exactly-zero samples before the first arrival and a near-zero fraction of up to 0.43 between spikes in the sparser rooms, while the pra RIRs have no zero or near-zero samples anywhere in the first 2000, the sinc kernels filling every sample. The autocorrelation FWHM is 1 sample for both tracks in all rooms, so both are spectrally flat to Nyquist at the segment level and the ACF width does not separate the two textures. At the profile level the tail means agree closely, but the shapes before the tail differ: pra profiles start at 1.0, reach their minimum within the first few dozen samples in six of eight rooms, and cross 50 percent of the rise within about 40 samples of it, while ab profiles start high (about 2.17, over the leading zeros), dip to minima between samples 229 and 4621, and take hundreds to thousands of samples to recover. The Criterion III crossings are strongly monotonically related (Spearman rho = 0.929) and the linear map ab = 1.58 * pra + 1718 explains R2 = 0.83 of the variance, the ab crossings sitting well above the pra ones in every room. These are descriptive observations on eight rooms and are not confirmatory evidence.
